# ADR-0003 — TV tutor via own app or controlled HDMI device (PROPOSED)

- Date: 2026-10-10.
- Recommendation: prioritize licensed content in owned Android TV/Google TV application; HDMI managed app fallback.
- Avoid claims of third-party Smart TV / YouTube takeover, unauthorized overlays or remote hidden interaction.
- Gate: actual device playback, quiz, captions, permission/legal review and per-platform support cost.
- State: proposed.
